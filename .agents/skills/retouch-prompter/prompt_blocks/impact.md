# Ударная гравировка (Impact Style)

All brightness values in this block are on a 0–255 scale.

1. **Face & Skin (Smooth Tonal Variation):**
   Render the skin smooth with subtle tonal micro-variation — even gradients with gentle tonal breathing. The skin must have sculptural volumetric depth through soft, even gradients with perceptible tonal shifts. Think of the skin as a fine watercolor wash — uniform at first glance, but with gentle tonal breathing up close. Eliminate all individual pores, freckle-scale spots, and digital noise. The engraving process amplifies surface texture — the file must be clean but not sterile. Skin must appear as though the surface was cleaned to a smooth, even base with all definition rendered through tonal transitions (light-to-shadow gradients). Any residual grain becomes permanent visible noise in the final engraved output. Skin should have a subtle 'silver luminosity' — semi-specular highlights on forehead, cheekbones and nose that suggest polished, living tissue.

2. **Hair Rendering (CRITICAL for Impact):**
   Render hair with DEFINED INDIVIDUAL STRANDS. White highlight streaks on hair take ABSOLUTE PRIORITY over dark strands. White highlights on stone read as engraved marks — even mid-gray areas appear dark in the final output. Generate PROMINENT WHITE HIGHLIGHT STREAKS following the hair growth direction, and minimize uniformly gray areas. Render dark strands as contrast elements against the face — direct tonal opposition. Hair must have clear SCULPTURAL VOLUME — it must look like a 3D mass with a distinct beginning, curve, and end. Render each lock with distinct volume and highlight streaks — flat, evenly-toned areas appear bald on engraved stone.

3. **Contrast & Edge Definition:**
   Render clothing and facial structure with macro-sharpness, ensuring every button, fabric weave, and fold is textured and clear. Make transitions deliberate and bold between shadow and light. Render dark clothing with visible fold highlights and fabric texture in the 60-90 brightness range. Keep the full tonal range of fabric folds readable.
