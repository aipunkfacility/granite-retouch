# Ударная гравировка (Impact Style)

1. **Face & Skin (Smooth Tonal Variation):**
   Render the skin SMOOTH with SUBTLE tonal micro-variation — NOT individual pores, NOT speckled texture, NOT mottled grain. The skin must look like a high-end beauty retouch that still retains a sense of living tissue: soft, even gradients with barely perceptible tonal shifts that prevent a "wax mannequin" look. Think of the skin as a fine watercolor wash — uniform at first glance, but with gentle tonal breathing up close. Eliminate all individual pores, freckle-scale spots, and digital noise. The needle will add its own texture on stone; the file must be clean but not sterile.

2. **Hair Rendering (CRITICAL for Impact):**
   Render hair with DEFINED INDIVIDUAL STRANDS. White highlight streaks on hair take ABSOLUTE PRIORITY over dark strands. For impact engraving, white = engraved, gray = already "dark" for the machine. Generate PROMINENT WHITE HIGHLIGHT STREAKS following the hair growth direction, and minimize uniformly gray areas. Dark strands should only appear where they contrast directly with the face. Avoid flat, evenly-toned hair — it will appear completely bald on the engraved stone.

3. **Contrast & Edge Definition:**
   Render clothing and facial structure with macro-sharpness, ensuring every button, fabric weave, and fold is textured and clear. The impact machine "loves" when shadow is clearly separated from light — make transitions deliberate and bold, not gradual. Utilize a bright **Inner Glow (10-25px)** around the subject's edges to ensure strong separation from the background, maximizing the readability of the physical engraving.

**Goal (Impact Optimization):** Create a high-contrast memorial portrait optimized for diamond needle impact engraving. Skin brightness 220-235 with smooth tonal variation (not flat, not speckled), bold highlight/shadow transitions, sculptural hair with DOMINANT white highlights, macro-sharp clothing details, and vibrant catchlights in the eyes to prevent a "plastic" appearance on the engraved surface. No skin area may exceed brightness 240 except eye whites.
