# Laser Style

The face is the dominant light element — luminous and bright, with sculptural volume from smooth light-to-shadow gradients. Render the skin as polished porcelain: clean of pores, noise, and grain, with flawless airbrushed transitions across all facial planes. Build sculptural volume through light and shadow, with smooth surface finish. Skin stays within tonal range — only the whites of the eyes reach pure white. The jawline matches the original photo exactly — it is the sharpest tonal boundary on the lower face, with distinct brightness between face and neck.

Hair matches the source photo in length and style. Render it as large sculptural locks with bold white highlight ridges following the growth direction. Highlights take absolute priority — even mid-gray reads dark on stone. Sculptural volume comes from highlight ridges defining individual locks, with clean separation between adjacent locks.

Render clothing with sculpted 3D volume through smooth, high-contrast shading. Sculpted volume takes priority over fine fabric texture.
