# Лазерная гравировка (Laser Style)

1. **Face & Skin (Porcelain Smoothness):**
   Render the skin with the materiality of smooth, polished porcelain. Eliminate all pores, wrinkles, and digital noise, replacing them with mathematically perfect light-to-shadow gradients. The skin must glow with soft, even highlights — a respectful, airbrushed aesthetic. The stone itself provides the final texture; the file must be clean. Avoid a "waxy" look by ensuring strong 3D volumetric depth through light and shadow, NOT through skin texture.

2. **Hair Rendering (Sculptural Volume):**
   Form hair into large, sculptural locks with PROMINENT WHITE HIGHLIGHT RIDGES and deep black shadow valleys. The highlights must be bold and follow the hair growth direction — this is what the laser reads on stone. Even gray-toned mid-values will appear "dark" on the engraved stone — therefore prioritize bright highlight streaks over uniform gray fill. Avoid flat, evenly-toned hair; it will appear bald on the final engraving. Avoid fine individual strands; instead, create bold, confident light/shadow masses.

3. **Materiality & Sculpted Volume:**
   To ensure edge-to-edge sharpness and prevent laser melting, capture clothing with sculpted 3D volume rather than fine fabric texture. Avoid imitating wool or grain; instead, convey the structure through smooth, high-contrast shading. Clothing must remain detailed and defined but rendered with smooth tonal transitions, not rough grain.

**Goal (Laser Optimization):** Create a professional memorial portrait with "High-End Airbrush" materiality. Skin brightness 230-245, porcelain-smooth with no pores or noise, sculptural hair with prominent white highlights, clean volumetric depth without harsh graphic lines. No skin area may exceed brightness 240 except eye whites. Use an **f/11 aperture** setting for maximum depth of field and sharpness.
