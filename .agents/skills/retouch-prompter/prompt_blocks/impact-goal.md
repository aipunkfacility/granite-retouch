## Goal (Impact Optimization)

Create a high-contrast memorial portrait optimized for diamond needle impact engraving. The face should be clearly bright in the illuminated zones — well above mid-gray — with distinct shadow zones below. Deep eye socket shadows preserve sculptural volume. Prioritize contrast over brightness. Bold highlight/shadow transitions, sculptural hair with dominant white highlights, rim light along the full silhouette, macro-sharp clothing details, and vibrant catchlights in the eyes.
