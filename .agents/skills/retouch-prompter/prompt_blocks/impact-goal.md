## Goal (Impact Optimization)

Create a high-contrast memorial portrait optimized for diamond needle impact engraving. The face reads as medium gray overall with illuminated zones well above mid-gray and shadow zones well below mid-gray. No single zone becomes a bright patch — even illumination across the face. Forehead wrinkles are prominent sculptural elements. Eye wrinkles and nasolabial folds are present and natural. Deep eye socket shadows preserve sculptural volume. Prioritize contrast over brightness. Bold highlight/shadow transitions, sculptural hair with dominant white highlights, clear gray rim light along the full silhouette, smooth fabric clothing details, and vibrant catchlights in the eyes.
