# Laser 80W Style

Render the skin smooth and even in base tone, but preserve major wrinkles as soft tonal transitions — they give the face character and naturalism on stone. Eliminate pores and noise, keep structural wrinkles. Build sculptural volume through light and shadow. Skin must never reach pure white — only eye whites may.

Hair matches the source photo in length and style. Render it as a dark sculptural mass with subtle highlight streaks following the growth direction. Hair must remain noticeably darker than the face. Highlight streaks define volume but must not dominate. Create sculptural locks with subtle light ridges instead of fine individual strands.

Render clothing with sculpted 3D volume through smooth, high-contrast shading and readable texture throughout. Flat areas without texture cause over-engraving on stone. Sculpted volume takes priority over fine fabric texture.
