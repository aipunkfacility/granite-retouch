# Constraints

Clothing stays below face brightness.
The lit face skin reads as medium gray — no broad bright patches on the forehead or cheeks.
Face shadow zones stay well below mid-gray — shadows must not lift toward the mid-tone range.
Anything visible on the body/face that is not clothing must be reproduced in the generated image.
Keep the jawline as the sharpest tonal boundary on the lower face, with distinct brightness between face and neck.
Deep eye socket shadows preserve sculptural volume on stone.
