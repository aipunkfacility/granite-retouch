# Civilian

Professionally replace the original outfit with formal civilian attire suitable for a memorial portrait. The new clothing — whether a refined blazer, suit jacket, or a respectful formal dress — must fit perfectly, maintaining the dignity and proportions of the subject. Focus on the sharp lines of lapels, collars, and defined detail for a polished, high-end look.

Render clothing in gray tonal ranges — solid white or solid black areas lose detail on stone. Prioritize detail over brightness — when detail and brightness conflict, render darker. Clothing stays below face brightness. Render clothing with clear tonal definition — the fabric structure must read clearly through tonal variation between light and shadow.

Render dark clothing with visible tonal transitions from fold structure throughout — the clothing reads as dimensional fabric, not a flat dark area. Keep dark clothing shadows above 0 except deep seam lines.

Render collar lines and lapel edges razor-sharp with clean 3D definition. Render each side of the collar individually according to perspective.
