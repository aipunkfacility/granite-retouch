# Civilian

Professionally replace the original outfit with formal civilian attire suitable for a memorial portrait. The new clothing — whether a refined blazer, suit jacket, or a respectful formal dress — must fit perfectly, maintaining the dignity and proportions of the subject. Focus on the sharp lines of lapels, collars, and defined detail for a polished, high-end look.

Render clothing in gray tonal ranges — solid white or solid black areas lose detail on stone. Match each garment's tonal range to the source. Prioritize texture detail over brightness — when detail and brightness conflict, render darker. Clothing stays below face brightness. Keep fabric texture sharp and detailed.

Render each side of the collar individually according to perspective.
