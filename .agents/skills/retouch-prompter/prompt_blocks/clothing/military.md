# Военная форма (Military)

1. **Military Service Architecture:**
   Replace the original clothing with a formal military service uniform. The new uniform must look sharp and official, with proper proportions for the shoulder boards (epaulettes) and collar insignia. Maintain the dignity of the military rank while ensuring the lines are clean and respectful. (Note: Include visible medals and insignia from the original photo where they are clearly readable. Exclude only tiny pins or details that would become unrecognizable noise at engraving scale — the client knows every medal by heart).

2. **Clothing Brightness Rule:**
   Ensure the uniform is NOT brighter than the face. If the uniform appears overexposed, darken it slightly. Preserve and sharpen every readable detail: shoulder boards, collar insignia, buttons, fabric folds, and medals.
