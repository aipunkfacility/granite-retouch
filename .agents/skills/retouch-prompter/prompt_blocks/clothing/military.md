# Военная форма (Military)

1. **Military Uniform Preservation:**
   Carefully preserve and enhance the military uniform from the source photo. Do NOT replace the uniform with a generic one — keep the exact type, cut, rank indicators, and proportions of the original. Restore fabric texture, repair blur and artifacts, and sharpen every military-specific detail. The uniform must look crisp with clear tonal definition, clearly defined against the subject's skin and the background. Render all medals, orders, and insignia exactly as visible in the source photo — the client knows every decoration by heart and will notice any omission or addition. Simplify only sub-pixel details that fall below engraving resolution.

2. **Clothing Tonal Rule:**
   Render clothing in gray tonal ranges — solid white or solid black areas lose detail on stone. Apply tonal mapping per garment based on the source (brightness 0–255 scale): light garments as light gray (brightness 160–200), medium garments as medium gray (brightness 100–160), dark garments as dark gray (brightness 40–100), very dark garments as charcoal gray (brightness 20–60). Include only the tonal ranges present in the source clothing — skip ranges with no corresponding garment. Prioritize texture detail over brightness — adjust tonal range to preserve visible detail. When detail and brightness conflict, render darker rather than lighter. Maintain visible detail even in the lightest zones — flat white areas without texture cause over-engraving on stone. Clothing must remain below face brightness — the face is the brightest element of the portrait. Preserve and sharpen every readable detail: shoulder boards, collar insignia, buttons, fabric folds, and medals.
   Render dark military uniforms with visible tonal transitions throughout. Fabric shadows at brightness 15–40, fabric highlights at 60–90. Keep dark clothing shadows above 0 except deep seam lines.

3. **Detail Preservation:**
   Preserve ALL readable military details from the original: shoulder boards (epaulettes), collar insignia, rank chevrons, medals, orders, ribbon bars, cap badges, buttons, embroidery, and stitching — the client knows every item by heart and will notice any omission. Do not add decorations that were not present in the source photo.

4. **Collar & Lapel Definition:**
   Render collar lines and lapel edges razor-sharp with clean 3D definition.
