# Сохранение одежды (Preserve)

1. **Clothing Architecture:**
   Carefully restore and enhance the original clothing found in the source photo. Your task is to maintain the exact cut, proportions, and authentic details of the original outfit while repairing any damage, blur, or artifacts. The resulting clothing must look crisp and high-contrast, clearly defined against the subject's skin and the background.

2. **Clothing Brightness Rule:**
   Ensure clothing is NOT brighter than the face. If clothing appears overexposed, darken it slightly — bright clothing produces a white "blowout" spot on engraved stone that draws attention away from the face. The clothing texture creates essential contrast with the smooth skin — do NOT blur or over-smooth the fabric. Preserve and sharpen every readable detail: folds, buttons, stitching, weave patterns.
