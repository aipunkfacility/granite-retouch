# Preserve

Render the original clothing matching the source photo exactly — same cut, proportions, and authentic details. The resulting clothing must look crisp with clear tonal definition, clearly defined against the subject's skin and the background.

Render clothing in gray tonal ranges — solid white or solid black areas lose detail on stone. Match each garment's tonal range to the source. Prioritize texture detail over brightness — when detail and brightness conflict, render darker. Clothing stays below face brightness. Keep fabric texture sharp and detailed — it creates essential contrast with smooth skin and provides readable fabric structure. Render all readable details from the original — the client knows every item by heart and will notice any omission.

Render dark clothing with visible tonal transitions throughout. Keep dark clothing shadows above 0 except deep seam lines.

Render collar lines and lapel edges razor-sharp with clean 3D definition.
