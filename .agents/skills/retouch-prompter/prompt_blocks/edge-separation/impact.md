## Edge Separation (Impact)

Add a rim light along the subject's contour — as though a light source behind the subject catches the edges of the face, ears, hair, shoulders, and clothing. Apply rim lighting tracing the silhouette on the subject's surface (illuminating skin, fabric edges, hair strands). Vary rim light intensity — stronger on the illuminated side, weaker on the shadow side. Render it as a natural backlight reflex from the main light source.
This applies to the silhouette edge only, not to facial features — facial transitions must remain smooth as defined in the Anti-Doll guideline.
The rim light on clothing edges reads just above the adjacent fabric tone — readable on stone while remaining visually connected to the fabric. The rim light must never reach pure white.
