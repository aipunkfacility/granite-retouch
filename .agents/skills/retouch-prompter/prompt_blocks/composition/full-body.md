# Полный рост (Full-Body)

1. **Full-Body Composition:**
   Preserve the full-body stance exactly as in the source photo. Maintain the exact proportions of the body — do not elongate, shorten, or distort any body part. The pose must read naturally and respectfully on stone. Ensure the feet and shoes are fully visible and sharply defined — cropped or ambiguous feet ruin the composition on engraved stone. The overall silhouette must be clean and unambiguous from head to toe.
