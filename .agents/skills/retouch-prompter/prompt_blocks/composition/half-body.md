# Поясной портрет (Half-Body)

Frame the subject from the waist up. Maintain exact body proportions — the torso is neither elongated nor compressed. The lower boundary of the frame is the waistline or belt. If hands are visible at waist level, preserve them exactly as in the source photo. If hands are hidden, maintain the natural drape of the clothing, rendering folds only. The waist is the clear framing boundary, with natural posture and dignified stance.
