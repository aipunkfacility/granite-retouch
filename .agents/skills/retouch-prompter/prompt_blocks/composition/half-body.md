# Поясной портрет (Half-Body)

1. **Half-Body Composition:**
   Frame the subject from the waist up. Maintain exact body proportions — do not elongate or compress the torso. The lower boundary of the frame is the waistline / belt. If hands are visible at waist level — preserve them exactly as in the source photo. If hands are not visible — do not invent them. The waist must be clearly defined as the framing boundary, with natural posture and dignified stance.
