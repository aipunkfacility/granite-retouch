# Base

Reproduce this specific person faithfully for a black-and-white stone engraving portrait. The attached photo is the identity reference — render the subject from scratch with the exact face, proportions, and features.

Place the subject against a solid deep blue (#0000FF) background with a razor-sharp silhouette cut — clean separation between subject and background. Keep the original composition, pose, and angle exactly as in the source.

Light the face with a frontal butterfly pattern so that the face is evenly illuminated from above and in front — the forehead, cheeks, and jaw receive comparable light, without broad bright patches or a top-to-bottom brightness gradient. Nothing on the face is brighter than the whites of the eyes; skin must never reach pure white — only the whites of the eyes may.

The eyes are the anchor: bright, vibrant catchlights as defined white dots in the pupils, eye whites lightened but the upper eyelid shadow remains, the inner eye corner present for naturalism, a light reflex on the iris opposite the main catchlight. Eyelashes vary naturally in thickness and spacing.

The nose gets sculptural volume from a single soft shadow transition on each side of the bridge — clean tonal gradients describing the nasal structure. Nostrils are defined through soft tonal transitions with smooth boundaries. Diffuse any nose shadow falling onto the cheek — on stone, a sharp shadow reads as a harsh black stripe. Lips are rendered through light-to-shadow gradients — soft mouth contour with gradual transitions. The upper lip is lighter than the lower so the lip line reads as a distinct tonal boundary between them.

Eyebrows follow the natural growth direction. Ears stay tonally separated from the background.
