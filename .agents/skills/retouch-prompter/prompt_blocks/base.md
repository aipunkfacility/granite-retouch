# Base

You are a master retoucher preparing a black-and-white portrait for diamond needle impact engraving on stone. The attached photo is your source — reproduce this specific person faithfully, not a generic face.

Place the subject against a solid deep blue (#0000FF) background with a razor-sharp silhouette cut. Keep the original composition, pose, and angle exactly as in the source.

The face is the heart of the image. Light it with a frontal butterfly pattern so that the upper face — forehead, cheekbones, nose bridge — reads as the illuminated side, and the lower sides — under-eyes, jawline, sides of the nose — fall into shadow. The illuminated zones of the face should be clearly bright — well above mid-gray — making the face the dominant light element of the portrait. This contrast between light and shadow makes the face sculptural on stone. The face must never look flat or porcelain — think of skin as a form turning under directional light, with clearly distinct light and shadow regions connected by smooth mid-tone gradients. Keep skin smooth and clean of pores, noise, and grain, but do not flatten the tonal range — the transitions between light and shadow must be visible and natural. Nothing on the face is brighter than the whites of the eyes; skin must never reach pure white — only the whites of the eyes may.

The eyes are the anchor: bright, vibrant catchlights as defined white dots in the pupils, eye whites lightened but with the upper eyelid shadow preserved (without it, eyes look glassy and dead). Keep the inner eye corner for naturalism — its absence makes a doll. A light reflex on the iris opposite the main catchlight, but keep it subdued. Eyelashes vary naturally in thickness and spacing.

The nose gets volume from soft shading transitions between the light and shadow sides — never two dark stripes along the bridge. Nostrils are subtly defined, not outlined. Soften any nose shadow falling onto the cheek — on stone, it becomes a harsh black stripe. Lips are rendered through light-to-shadow gradients only — no dark contour lines around the mouth. The upper lip is slightly lighter than the lower so the lip line doesn't merge into a dark band.

Eyebrows follow the natural growth direction. Wrinkles are soft tonal suggestions — deep dark lines look angry or sick on stone. Nasolabial folds are gentle tonal gradients, never painted grooves. The jawline matches the original photo exactly. Ears stay tonally separated from the background.
