# Final Prompt for ORD-2026-005

**Role & Context:**
You are an academic artist specializing in memorial stone engraving. Your goal is to create an idealized, sculptural rendering of TWO subjects (woman and man in military uniform) from the input photo, intended for CNC laser engraving on black granite (gabbro). You are NOT creating a photograph; you are producing a clean, high-contrast, engraving-ready graphic base.

**Aesthetic Standard (Style Guide: Memorial High-End Airbrush):**
Follow these standards strictly: prioritize airbrushed smoothness, sculpted 3D volume, and clean, vector-sharp facial features, maintaining a professional memorial portrait look.

**Strict Execution Guidelines:**

1. **Identity Preservation (CRITICAL PRIORITY):**
   * Maintain the identity, expression, and bone structure of BOTH subjects exactly as in the original photo.
   * Do NOT change facial features. Proportions are strictly preserved.

2. **Background (Blue Screen Separation):**
   * **Color:** Strictly **Solid Deep Blue (#0000FF)** background.
   * **Constraints:** NO gradients, NO light effects, NO objects. Just pure blue.
   * **Edges:** The silhouettes of both subjects must be razor-sharp against the blue. NO white halos.

3. **Materiality & Texture (Laser Optimized):**
   * **Face & Skin (Both):** Sophisticated, nuanced skin texture. Retain subtle, natural micro-details (like refined skin pores) to avoid a "waxy" appearance, while keeping the overall surface clean and smooth. Use soft, intelligent gradients of light and shadow to create 3D volume.
   * **Hair & Headgear:** 
     * Woman: Maintain exact bob haircut volume, smooth shapes, strong contrast, with subtle, natural hair texture.
     * Man: Maintain military cap volume and shape. Render as a bold, clean form with strong contrast and subtle material definition.
   * **Clothing:** 
     * Woman: Crisp, high-contrast civilian attire. 
     * Man: Precise, high-contrast military uniform details. 
     * Subtle, realistic fabric texture is permitted to add depth, but NO digital noise or coarse grain. Focus on sharp, clear lines for folds and details.

4. **Lighting & Contrast (Chiaroscuro):**
   * **Style:** Rigid, sculptural studio lighting (frontal) that feels natural yet professional.
   * **Highlights/Shadows:** Deep, clean shadows and bright, pure white highlights on forehead, nose, cheekbones, and chin for both subjects to ensure 3D depth.
   * **Eyes:** Bright, vibrant. Whites are pure. Reflections in pupils are perfect white dots.

**Negative Constraints (Forbidden):**
* **NO Hyper-Photorealism:** Avoid excessive skin details, harsh blemishes, or deep, distracting wrinkles.
* **NO Digital Noise/Grain:** No coarse grain or "dirty" artifacts.
* **NO Painting Style:** No brush strokes, no cartoon effects, no GTA-style.
* **NO 3D-Render Artifacts:** The result must NOT look like a game character or plastic doll. It must look like high-end professional studio retouching for stone engraving.

**Final Goal:**
A production-ready, engraving-ready graphic portrait of two subjects with deep blue background, suitable for laser engraving on black stone.
