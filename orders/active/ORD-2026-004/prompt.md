# Final Prompt for ORD-2026-004

**Role & Context:**
You are an academic artist specializing in memorial stone engraving. Your goal is to create an idealized, sculptural rendering of the subject, intended for CNC laser engraving on black granite (gabbro). You are NOT creating a photograph; you are producing a clean, high-contrast, engraving-ready graphic base.

**Aesthetic Standard (Style Guide: Memorial High-End Airbrush):**
Follow these standards strictly: prioritize airbrushed smoothness, sculpted 3D volume, and clean, vector-sharp facial features, maintaining a professional memorial portrait look.

**Strict Execution Guidelines:**

1. **Identity Preservation (CRITICAL PRIORITY):**
   * Keep the subject's identity, expression, and bone structure exact.
   * Maintain the precise shape of nose, eyes, lips, and jawline. This is a restoration of *this specific person*.

2. **Background (Blue Screen Separation):**
   * **Color:** Strictly **Solid Deep Blue (#0000FF)** background.
   * **Constraints:** NO gradients, NO light effects, NO objects. Just pure blue.
   * **Edges:** The silhouette must be razor-sharp against the blue. NO white halos.

3. **Materiality & Texture (Laser Optimized):**
   * **Face & Skin:** Absolutely smooth, porcelain skin. NO pores, NO wrinkles, NO spots, NO micro-texture. Use soft, mathematically perfect gradients of light and shadow to create volume.
   * **Hair & Headgear:** MUST be short, close-cropped hair (military style, almost shaved). Define clear, structured volume following the skull's curvature. NO "fluffiness", NO long strands, NO volume beyond the natural scalp line. Render as bold, clean forms with strong contrast to define the silhouette against the blue background.
   * **Clothing:** Crisply rendered, high-contrast. NO fabric simulation (no tweed, linen, or woolen texture). NO digital noise or grain. Focus on sharp, clear lines for folds and details.

4. **Lighting & Contrast (Chiaroscuro):**
   * **Style:** Rigid, sculptural studio lighting (frontal).
   * **Highlights:** Pure white (High Key) on forehead, bridge of nose, cheekbones, chin.
   * **Shadows:** Deep, clean shadows in eye sockets, under nose, and chin to ensure 3D depth.
   * **Eyes:** Bright, vibrant. Whites are pure. Reflections in pupils are perfect white dots.

**Negative Constraints (Forbidden):**
* **NO Photorealism:** No pores, no wrinkles, no skin blemishes.
* **NO Digital Noise/Grain:** No texture imitation, no "dirty" artifacts.
* **NO Painting Style:** No brush strokes, no cartoon effects, no GTA-style.
* **NO 3D-Render Artifacts:** The result must NOT look like a game character or wax doll. It must look like high-end professional studio retouching for stone engraving.

**Final Goal:**
A production-ready, engraving-ready graphic portrait optimized for laser engraving on black stone.
